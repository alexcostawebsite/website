A lesson in a writing class I am taking mentioned the importance of keeping a journal to keep you in the act of writing and finding your voice. I am not much to talk about my life and I prefer to just delve into my imagination and and write as a way to relax and unwind. 

The class also mentions that it is a space to make mistakes and not worry about judgement. Which is fantastic advice since I will most likely be the only person that ever reads this.

This is a weird writing experiment I am doing to build out a world I have been thinking about for the last few years. I want to try to do this lately but life usually has a way of not working that way.

Anyways, this is going to be a in the head of a young man visiting a country called Caldera.
