
# Writing 101: The Technical Bits

## Summary
I was watching a conversation on Discord amongst other writers. Hearing them use things like "predicate" and other technical bits made me realize a few things. 

I do not know any of the technical bits.

Coming at writing the way I currently do it means I am lacking crucial tools on the things that make sentences, that make paragraphs, and that fundamentally shape the larger structure of writing. Because of this I have decided to make a self paced course for my self on these parts of writing.

## Daily tasks for the 6 week course

### Week 1 — Sentence Skeletons

**Main book:** _The English Grammar Workbook for Adults_  
**Goal:** Understand how sentences work.

#### Day 1 — Parts of speech

Read: Klinkenborg 2–4 pages + Grammar Workbook section on parts of speech.

Drill: Take 10 sentences from one of your stories and label nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs.

Apply: Rewrite 5 sentences using stronger verbs.

Minimum: Label nouns and verbs in 5 sentences.

---

#### Day 2 — Subjects and predicates

Read: Klinkenborg + Grammar Workbook section on subjects and predicates.

Drill: Label the subject and predicate in 10 sentences from one of your stories.

Apply: Fix 5 sentences where the subject or action feels unclear.

Minimum: Label 5 sentences.

---

#### Day 3 — Main verbs

Read: Klinkenborg + Grammar Workbook section on verbs.

Drill: Find the main verb in 15 sentences.

Apply: Replace 5 weak verbs.

Example:

> acted like lullabies → lulled

Minimum: Find the main verb in 5 sentences.

---

#### Day 4 — Objects

Read: Klinkenborg + Grammar Workbook section on direct and indirect objects.

Drill: Find the object in 10 sentences.

Apply: Rewrite 5 sentences so the action lands more clearly.

Minimum: Label subject, verb, and object in 5 sentences.

---

##### Day 5 — Phrases

Read: Klinkenborg + Grammar Workbook section on phrases.

Drill: Identify prepositional phrases and participial phrases in your draft.

Apply: Cut or move 5 phrases that make sentences clunky.

Minimum: Find 5 prepositional phrases.

---

#### Day 6 — Clauses

Read: Klinkenborg + Grammar Workbook section on independent and dependent clauses.

Drill: Mark independent and dependent clauses in 10 sentences.

Apply: Fix any fragments, run-ons, or comma splices you notice.

Minimum: Mark clauses in 5 sentences.

---

#### Day 7 — Sentence skeleton review

No new book section unless you want one.

Task: Take the first 500 words of Anthony/Chapman and label:

- Subject
- Predicate
- Main verb
- Object
- Phrase
- Clause

Apply: Rewrite the weakest paragraph.

Minimum: Rewrite one paragraph.

---

### Week 2 — Clarity and Concision

**Main books:** _The Elements of Style_ + _The Elements of Style Workbook_  
**Goal:** Make sentences cleaner.

#### Day 8 — Omit needless words

Read: Klinkenborg + _Elements of Style_ section “Omit needless words.”

Drill: Cut 50 words from your Anthony opening without changing meaning.

Apply: Rewrite 10 bloated sentences.

Minimum: Cut 20 words.

---

#### Day 9 — Active voice

Read: Klinkenborg + active voice section.

Drill: Find 10 passive, weak, or indirect constructions.

Apply: Rewrite them more directly.

Minimum: Rewrite 3 sentences.

---

#### Day 10 — Concrete language

Read: Klinkenborg + concrete language section.

Drill: Find 10 vague words or abstract phrases.

Apply: Replace them with specific images or actions.

Example:

> He wanted more.

Could become:

> He had started checking apartment listings he could not afford.

Minimum: Replace 5 vague phrases.

---

#### Day 11 — Positive form

Read: Klinkenborg + “Put statements in positive form.”

Drill: Find 5 negative or roundabout sentences.

Apply: Rewrite them directly.

Minimum: Rewrite 3 sentences.

---

#### Day 12 — Keep related words together

Read: Klinkenborg + “Keep related words together.”

Drill: Find 5 sentences where a phrase is too far from the word it modifies.

Apply: Fix the sentence structure.

Minimum: Fix 2 sentences.

---

#### Day 13 — Workbook practice

Read: Klinkenborg.

Task: Complete 1–2 exercises from _The Elements of Style Workbook_.

Apply: Choose one rule from the workbook and apply it to 250 words of your draft.

Minimum: Complete one workbook page.

---

#### Day 14 — Weekly style pass

Task: Revise the first 750 words of your Anthony/Chapman opening for style.

Checklist:

- Cut needless words
- Strengthen verbs
- Use concrete language
- Break overloaded sentences
- Read aloud

Minimum: Revise 250 words.

---

### Week 3 — Punctuation and Sentence Boundaries

**Main book:** _Eats, Shoots & Leaves_  
**Goal:** Understand punctuation as rhythm and meaning.

#### Day 15 — Apostrophes

Read: Klinkenborg + apostrophe section.

Drill: Write 10 possessive examples.

Apply: Check your draft for apostrophe errors.

Minimum: Check one page.

---

#### Day 16 — Commas

Read: Klinkenborg + comma section.

Drill: Take one page of your draft. Mark every comma and explain why it is there.

Apply: Remove, add, or move commas as needed.

Minimum: Review commas in 250 words.

---

#### Day 17 — Run-ons and comma splices

Read: Klinkenborg + Grammar Workbook review on clauses if needed.

Drill: Find any sentence boundaries that feel wrong.

Apply: Fix them using:

- Period
- Semicolon
- Conjunction
- Restructuring

Minimum: Fix 3 sentence-boundary issues.

---

#### Day 18 — Semicolons and colons

Read: Klinkenborg + semicolon/colon section.

Drill: Write 5 semicolon sentences and 5 colon sentences.

Apply: Use one semicolon or colon in your draft only if it improves the rhythm.

Minimum: Write 2 semicolon examples.

---

#### Day 19 — Dashes

Read: Klinkenborg + dash section.

Drill: Rewrite 5 sentences three ways:

1. With a comma
2. With a dash
3. With a period

Apply: Choose the version with the best rhythm.

Minimum: Rewrite 2 sentences three ways.

---

#### Day 20 — Dialogue punctuation

Read: Klinkenborg + review dialogue punctuation rules.

Drill: Take 10 lines of Anthony/Chapman dialogue and check punctuation.

Apply: Fix tags, beats, commas, and periods.

Minimum: Fix one dialogue exchange.

---

#### Day 21 — Weekly punctuation pass

Task: Edit 1,000 words for punctuation only.

Do not revise story, style, or emotion today. Only punctuation.

Minimum: Edit 500 words.

---

### Week 4 — Showing, Telling, and Exposition

**Main book:** _Self-Editing for Fiction Writers_  
**Goal:** Make the scene less explained and more dramatized.

#### Day 22 — Show and tell

Read: Klinkenborg + _Self-Editing_ chapter on show and tell.

Drill: Find 10 places where you explain emotion, motive, or meaning.

Apply: Rewrite 5 as behavior, image, dialogue, or action.

Minimum: Rewrite 2 telling sentences.

---

#### Day 23 — Exposition

Read: Klinkenborg + exposition section.

Drill: Highlight all backstory in the opening.

Apply: Cut, move, or dramatize 30% of it.

Minimum: Cut 100 words of exposition.

---

#### Day 24 — Interior monologue

Read: Klinkenborg + interior monologue section.

Drill: Find 5 places where Anthony’s thoughts explain too much.

Apply: Rewrite those thoughts so they feel more character-filtered.

Minimum: Rewrite 2 thoughts.

---

#### Day 25 — Characterization

Read: Klinkenborg + characterization section.

Drill: Make a four-column list for Anthony:

- What he says
- What he does
- What he thinks
- What he avoids

Apply: Add one small behavior that reveals his dissatisfaction without saying “he wanted more.”

Minimum: Add one revealing detail.

---

#### Day 26 — Point of view

Read: Klinkenborg + POV section.

Drill: Mark any sentence that feels outside Anthony’s awareness.

Apply: Rewrite those lines through Anthony’s perception.

Minimum: Check 250 words for POV consistency.

---

#### Day 27 — Beats

Read: Klinkenborg + beats section.

Drill: Find 5 “said while” or awkward dialogue-action constructions.

Apply: Replace them with cleaner beats.

Example:

> “Yes,” the man said while taking off his gloves.

Becomes:

> “Yes.” The man removed his gloves finger by finger.

Minimum: Fix 3 beats.

---

#### Day 28 — Weekly fiction edit

Task: Revise the full Anthony/Chapman opening for:

- Less backstory
- More scene
- Stronger character behavior
- Less emotional explanation
- Cleaner dialogue beats

Minimum: Revise 500 words.

---

### Week 5 — Dialogue, Voice, and Scene Pressure

**Main book:** _Self-Editing for Fiction Writers_  
**Support:** _The Elements of Style_  
**Goal:** Make Anthony and Chapman sound distinct and make the scene more tense.

#### Day 29 — Dialogue purpose

Read: Klinkenborg + dialogue section.

Drill: For every line of dialogue in one page, write what the speaker wants.

Apply: Cut or revise any line with no purpose.

Minimum: Analyze 10 lines.

---

#### Day 30 — Dialogue tags

Read: Klinkenborg + speaker tag/beat guidance.

Drill: Remove unnecessary tags from one page.

Apply: Replace weak tags with clean action beats only where useful.

Minimum: Clean one dialogue exchange.

---

#### Day 31 — Subtext

Read: Klinkenborg.

Drill: Write:

- What Anthony wants but will not say
- What Chapman wants but will not say

Apply: Revise one exchange so neither character directly says the real thing.

Minimum: Rewrite 10 lines of dialogue.

---

#### Day 32 — Power shift

Read: Klinkenborg.

Drill: Mark who has power at the start, middle, and end of the scene.

Apply: Make the power shift clearer through dialogue, silence, action, or knowledge.

Minimum: Identify the power shift.

---

#### Day 33 — Voice distinction

Read: Klinkenborg.

Drill: Make two word banks.

Anthony words/phrases: casual, defensive, sarcastic, modern.  
Chapman words/phrases: formal, transactional, calm, old-fashioned.

Apply: Rewrite 10 lines so the speakers sound less interchangeable.

Minimum: Rewrite 5 lines.

---

#### Day 34 — Chapter-ending hooks

Read: Klinkenborg.

Drill: Write 5 possible chapter endings.

Examples:

- Anthony remembers dying.
- Chapman reveals Anthony already agreed.
- The train doors open somewhere impossible.
- Anthony sees someone dead waiting on the platform.
- Chapman gives him his first assignment.

Apply: Pick one and draft toward it.

Minimum: Write 3 possible endings.

---

#### Day 35 — Weekly dialogue and scene pass

Task: Revise the full subway conversation.

Checklist:

- Each line has purpose
- Anthony and Chapman sound distinct
- Dialogue reveals power
- Beats are clean
- Subtext exists
- Scene ends with a hook

Minimum: Revise one full exchange.

---

### Week 6 — Final Revision and Transfer Test

**Main books:** all of them  
**Goal:** Produce a cleaner chapter and prove the skills transfer.

#### Day 36 — Grammar pass

Use: _English Grammar Workbook_

Task: Check the chapter for:

- Fragments
- Run-ons
- Subject-verb agreement
- Confusing sentence skeletons
- Awkward clauses

Minimum: Check 500 words.

---

#### Day 37 — Klinkenborg sentence pass

Use: _Several Short Sentences About Writing_

Task: Read your chapter sentence by sentence.

For each weak sentence, ask:

- Is it trying to do too much?
- Is the subject clear?
- Is the verb strong?
- Does it earn its length?
- Would it be better shorter?

Revise 20 sentences.

Minimum: Revise 10 sentences.

---

#### Day 38 — Style pass

Use: _The Elements of Style_

Task: Cut 10% of the chapter.

Focus on:

- Needless words
- Weak verbs
- Abstract phrasing
- Repeated ideas
- Overexplained emotion

Minimum: Cut 100 words.

---

#### Day 39 — Punctuation pass

Use: _Eats, Shoots & Leaves_

Task: Check:

- Commas
- Apostrophes
- Dialogue punctuation
- Dashes
- Semicolons
- Sentence boundaries

Minimum: Check one page.

---

#### Day 40 — Fiction craft pass

Use: _Self-Editing for Fiction Writers_

Task: Check:

- Show/tell
- Exposition
- Dialogue
- Beats
- POV
- Interior monologue
- Scene pressure

Minimum: Fix 3 fiction-craft issues.

---

#### Day 41 — Read-aloud pass

Task: Read the full chapter aloud.

Mark every place where you:

- Trip over a sentence
- Get bored
- Hear repetition
- Lose track of meaning
- Feel the rhythm go flat
- Hear dialogue that sounds unnatural

Fix only what you marked.

Minimum: Read aloud 500 words.

---

#### Day 42 — Final exam

Write a new **1,000–1,500 word scene** from scratch.

Prompt:

> A fraud is forced to perform the real version of the fake service they sell.

Then do three passes:

1. Label subjects and predicates in 10 sentences.
2. Cut 10% of the draft.
3. Fix 5 show/tell issues.

Minimum: Write 500 new words and revise 5 sentences.
