Exercises from [Steering the Craft](https://www.ursulakleguin.com/steering-the-craft)

Sound of Writing
"An awareness of what your own writing sounds like is an essential skill for a writer."

- [x] Exercise One - Being Gorgeous: Write a paragraph that is meant to be read aloud. Make it rhythmic but do not make it a poem.
	- [ ] He flicked a coin from each hand. When they met in the air, the chirp they made became a sizzling vibration as they flipped back down. He called the trick Cicada Charmers. An accurate name for a trick that left a tickling in my ears.
- [x] Exercise Two:  Describe an action or a person feeling strong emotion. Try to make the rhythm represent the physical reality of the emotion.
	- [ ] She ping-ponged around the room like a fly buzzing around a picnic. Her eyes were glazed over as each rogue thought, each consequence, and each possible outcome played out. When she got this way she flicked her middle finger against the palm of her hand. Scratching motions that flicked each scene for another one.
