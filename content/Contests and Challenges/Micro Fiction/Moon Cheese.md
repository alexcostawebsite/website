	From dark until dawn can be a long, long way, especially through the dreary nights of winter. Tell us the story of a long winter’s night, true or made up, but make it short: fifty words or less.

“Good morning, moon,” her small voice squeaked as we walked inside.  
“It’s good night, moon,” I said as I brushed the snow from her jacket and kissed her cold forehead.  
“Not for the moon.” She grumbled. “The best cheese is made at night.”
