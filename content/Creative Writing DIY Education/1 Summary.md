# DIY Creative Writing Education

## The Summary
I am not a fan of college. If I had a large expendable income maybe I would go to college for creative writing. However, I do not and therefore I want to get better at creative writing without spending a large sum of money.
I have gotten the career I have now because of mostly self teaching myself with books and tutorials. So like how I tackled tech I want to tackle my creativity the same way.

Every resource on this page should not break the bank for most people.
